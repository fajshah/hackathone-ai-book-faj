## Scope

The project encompasses a Docusaurus-based AI-native textbook titled “Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics – Embodied Intelligence in the Real World.” The textbook will be generated by combining 4 modules, each containing 3 chapters. Each chapter will feature detailed explanations, examples, AI-generated diagrams, and professional academic formatting.

**Modules & Chapters:**
1. Module 1 — Introduction to Physical AI
   - Chapter 1: What is Physical AI?
   - Chapter 2: Embodiment: The Core Concept
   - Chapter 3: Components of a Physical AI System
2. Module 2 — Basics of Humanoid Robotics
   - Chapter 1: Introduction to Humanoids
   - Chapter 2: Robot Kinematics (FK & IK)
   - Chapter 3: Locomotion & Balance
3. Module 3 — ROS 2 Fundamentals
   - Chapter 1: What is ROS 2?
   - Chapter 2: Creating a ROS 2 Package
   - Chapter 3: Sensors & Actuators in ROS 2
4. Module 4 — Digital Twin Simulation (Gazebo & Webots)
   - Chapter 1: Introduction to Digital Twins
   - Chapter 2: Gazebo Basics
   - Chapter 3: Webots for Humanoid Simulation

Key features include a modern Docusaurus UI, "Select-text → Ask AI" integration, a RAG chatbot using Qdrant + Neon + FastAPI, optional Urdu translation mode, and a free-tier-friendly architecture.

## User Scenarios & Testing

### User Story 1 - Learning Foundations of Physical AI (Priority: P1)

As a student, I want to understand the core concepts of Physical AI and Embodied Intelligence so that I can grasp the foundational knowledge for robotics.

**Why this priority**: Essential for all subsequent learning.

**Independent Test**: Can be fully tested by a student reading the "Introduction to Physical AI & Embodied Intelligence" chapter and being able to explain key concepts.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1.  **Given** a student accesses the textbook, **When** they navigate to Chapter 1, **Then** they can read and comprehend the introduction to Physical AI and Embodied Intelligence.
2.  **Given** a student has read Chapter 1, **When** they are asked basic questions about Physical AI, **Then** they can answer them correctly.

---

### User Story 2 - Building ROS 2 Robot Controllers (Priority: P1)

As a student, I want to learn how to build and control robots using ROS 2 so that I can develop practical robotic applications.

**Why this priority**: ROS 2 is a core technology for robotics in this textbook.

**Independent Test**: Can be fully tested by a student completing the ROS 2 fundamentals lab and successfully controlling a simulated robot.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1.  **Given** a student has completed Chapter 3 and Module 1, **When** they attempt the ROS 2 lab, **Then** they can create ROS nodes, topics, and services to control a simulated robot.
2.  **Given** a student is working on a ROS 2 project, **When** they use `rclpy` with Python agents, **Then** they can successfully integrate Python code with their ROS 2 system.

---

### User Story 3 - Utilizing the RAG Chatbot (Priority: P2)

As a student, I want to use the integrated RAG chatbot to ask questions about the textbook content so that I can get immediate, accurate answers to clarify concepts.

**Why this priority**: Enhances learning experience and provides immediate support.

**Independent Test**: Can be fully tested by a student asking questions related to various chapters and receiving accurate, book-text-only answers.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1.  **Given** a student is reading any chapter, **When** they select text and choose "Ask AI", **Then** the chatbot provides a relevant answer derived only from the textbook content.
2.  **Given** a student asks a question not covered in the textbook, **When** they use the chatbot, **Then** the chatbot indicates it cannot find the answer in the book or gracefully responds.

---

### User Story 4 - Deploying the Textbook (Priority: P3)

As a developer/educator, I want to easily deploy the textbook to GitHub Pages so that it is publicly accessible to students.

**Why this priority**: Ensures the textbook is available for its intended audience.

**Independent Test**: Can be fully tested by successfully deploying the Docusaurus site to GitHub Pages.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1.  **Given** the Docusaurus project is built, **When** deployment to GitHub Pages is initiated, **Then** the textbook is published and accessible via a public URL.
2.  **Given** the textbook is deployed, **When** a user accesses the site, **Then** the UI is clean and functional.

## Book Layout / Content Structure

The textbook will be organized into 7 high-level chapters, each designed to progress the learner through foundational concepts to advanced applications in Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics. The content from the 4 conceptual modules (ROS 2, Digital Twin, NVIDIA Isaac, VLA) will be distributed and expanded across these chapters. Each module's core curriculum will be broken down into approximately 3 detailed sub-chapters (or major sections) within the overarching chapter structure, ensuring a granular and in-depth learning experience.

**Mapping of Modules to Chapters (Conceptual Integration):**
*   **Module 1 – The Robotic Nervous System (ROS 2):** Primarily integrated into Chapter 3 ("ROS 2 Fundamentals"), with relevant foundational aspects potentially touched upon in Chapter 1 or 2. This module's content will be expanded into 3 sub-chapters covering:
    *   ROS Nodes, Topics, Services: Architecture and communication patterns.
    *   `rclpy` integration with Python agents: Programming ROS 2 in Python.
    *   URDF for humanoids, ROS packages, launch files, parameters: Robot description and project setup.
*   **Module 2 – The Digital Twin (Gazebo & Unity):** Primarily integrated into Chapter 4 ("Digital Twin Simulation"), with connections to robotics basics in Chapter 2. This module's content will be expanded into 3 sub-chapters covering:
    *   Physics simulation (gravity, collisions): Core simulation principles.
    *   Sensor simulation (LiDAR, Depth, IMU): Replicating real-world perception.
    *   Unity visualization pipeline, SDF/URDF workflows: Advanced simulation and visualization.
*   **Module 3 – The AI-Robot Brain (NVIDIA Isaac):** Primarily integrated into Chapter 5 ("The AI-Robot Brain"), with advanced concepts flowing into Chapter 6 and 7. This module's content will be expanded into 3 sub-chapters covering:
    *   Isaac Sim photorealistic simulation: Advanced simulation for AI.
    *   Isaac ROS VSLAM + hardware acceleration: Perception and spatial understanding.
    *   Navigation (Nav2) for biped movement, Perception pipelines: AI-driven movement and environmental understanding.
*   **Module 4 – Vision-Language-Action (VLA):** Primarily integrated into Chapter 6 ("Vision-Language-Action Systems"), with the capstone project in Chapter 7 building upon these concepts. This module's content will be expanded into 3 sub-chapters covering:
    *   Whisper for Voice-to-Action, LLM cognitive planning → ROS action graphs: Language understanding and AI planning.
    *   Multimodal perception (camera + language): Integrating sensory inputs.
    *   Full pipeline for “Clean the room,” Capstone: Command → Plan → Perceive → Navigate → Manipulate: End-to-end VLA system development.


## Design Style & Tone

The textbook content will adhere to a professional academic style, specifically tailored for university-level learning in robotics and AI. This includes:
*   **Professional Academic Style**: Content will be formal, objective, and scholarly.
*   **Robotics + AI Terminology**: Precise and accurate use of domain-specific vocabulary.
*   **Consistency**: Uniformity in terminology, formatting, and presentation across all chapters.
*   **Clarity**: Explanations will be unambiguous and easy to understand for the target audience.
*   **Depth**: Concepts will be explored with sufficient detail to provide a solid understanding, suitable for advanced learners.

## Optional Features

- **Glossary**: A comprehensive glossary of key robotics and AI terms will be included at the end of the textbook.
- **Index and Cross-references**: A detailed index and cross-references between modules and chapters will be provided to enhance navigability.
- **Real-world Examples & Case Studies**: Integration of relevant real-world examples and case studies to provide applied learning context.

### Key Entities

- **Module**: A high-level organizational unit of the textbook, combining related concepts and containing 3 chapters.
- **Chapter**: A sub-section within a Module, providing detailed academic content on a specific topic.
- **Lab**: A practical exercise or project designed to reinforce learning outcomes.
- **RAG Chatbot**: An AI-powered conversational agent that retrieves information exclusively from the textbook content.
